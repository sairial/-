About Monster Survivors

Monster Survivors is an action-packed online game where you must survive waves of monstrous creatures. Use your skills and strategy to defeat the hordes and become the ultimate survivor.

How to Play

Control your character using the arrow keys or WASD. Attack monsters by clicking or tapping on them. Survive as long as possible to achieve a high score!

Game Features

  • Fast-paced action gameplay
  • Multiple levels of difficulty
  • Variety of monsters to defeat
  • Leaderboard to compete with friends
